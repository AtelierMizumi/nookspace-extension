## 2. Detailed Description (Formatted for Chrome Web Store)

```markdown
Transform your browser's New Tab into a calm, powerful, and deeply customizable productivity dashboard.

NookSpace brings your tasks, notes, bookmarks, study sessions, and daily goals into a unified workspace that lives entirely on your computer. Inspired by Notion and Linear, NookSpace gives you full control over your workflow with zero clutter, zero telemetry, and zero mandatory cloud accounts.

================================================================
KEY FEATURES AT A GLANCE
================================================================

▸ CUSTOMIZABLE DRAG-AND-DROP DASHBOARD
Arrange your workspace exactly how you think. Resize, drag, and snap widgets on a responsive 12-column grid. Switch seamlessly between Desktop, Tablet, and Compact layouts.

▸ MULTIPLE DEDICATED WORKSPACES
Create distinct spaces for Work, Study, Development, and Personal life. Switch instantly without tab clutter. Includes starter presets like Minimal Focus, Study & Exam, and Developer Dashboard.

▸ SMART BOOKMARKS ORGANIZER
Tame your browser bookmarks with custom folders, instant multi-token search, favicon previews, and two-way note linking. Import and export standard Netscape HTML bookmarks with one click.

▸ LOCAL-FIRST NOTES & KNOWLEDGE SYSTEM
Write notes, thoughts, and documentation in a distraction-free Markdown editor. Organize with collections, normalized tags, and bidirectional links to your saved bookmarks.

▸ INTEGRATED PRODUCTIVITY SUITE
• Pomodoro Timer: Background-accurate focus and break timers with desktop alerts.
• Study Tracker: Stopwatch logging focus minutes and tracking daily progress.
• Daily Goals: Set targets and celebrate daily achievements.
• Study Calendar & Streak: Visual activity heatmaps derived dynamically from completed work.

▸ DATA SOVEREIGNTY & UNIVERSAL JSON BACKUP
Your data belongs exclusively to you. Export your entire workspace into a clean, human-readable JSON backup at any time. Restore transactional backups with automatic rollback protection, or use Merge Import to selectively import data without overwriting existing workspaces.

================================================================
OUR PRIVACY-FIRST PLEDGE
================================================================

Unlike many modern extensions, NookSpace does not harvest your personal data:
✔ ZERO mandatory cloud accounts or signups
✔ ZERO remote servers or backend databases
✔ ZERO telemetry, tracking pixels, or analytics beacons
✔ ZERO browsing history or webpage inspection
✔ 100% of your data stays locally inside your browser's IndexedDB

================================================================
MINIMAL PERMISSIONS — PRINCIPLE OF LEAST PRIVILEGE
================================================================

NookSpace requests only the bare minimum permissions necessary:
• storage: For local theme and workspace synchronization across open tabs.
• alarms: To run background Pomodoro timers accurately during tab suspension.
• notifications: To alert you when your focus or break period finishes.

NookSpace never asks for broad host permissions (<all_urls>), browser history, or access to your browsing tabs.

================================================================
SUPPORT & COMMUNITY
================================================================
```
